# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** March 12, 2026  
**Project:** Resource Booking System - Phase 4

## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide security for the site's owners.

## 2. How We Use Cookies
The **Resource Booking System** uses cookies exclusively for functional and security purposes. We do not use cookies for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

## 3. The Cookies We Set
Based on our security audit (Phase 4), the following cookies are essential for the operation of the application:

| Cookie Name | Source | Purpose | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `csrf_token` | First-party | Prevents Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks by validating requests. | Session |
| `session` | First-party | Maintains your authenticated state (keeps you logged in). | Session |

## 4. Why These Cookies Are Necessary
* **Security:** Without the `csrf_token`, the application cannot verify that a request is coming from a legitimate user, exposing the system to malicious actions.
* **Functionality:** Without session cookies, the system would not "remember" you as you navigate from the resource list to the booking page, requiring you to log in for every single click.

## 5. Your Choices
Because these cookies are strictly necessary for the security and core functionality of the service, they do not require prior user consent under GDPR/ePrivacy Directive. However, you can choose to block or delete cookies through your browser settings, but this will result in the application being unable to function (you will not be able to log in or book resources).

## 6. More Information
If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact the System Administrator.
